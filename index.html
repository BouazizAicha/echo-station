      Belu finds his friends with echoes. Scientists do the same with the Moon! In 1969, the Apollo 11 astronauts left a mirror there. Fire a laser at it… and wait for the echo.

      Echo received: seconds. That is how long light takes to go to the Moon and back right now.

      The mirror has no battery and no engine. It just sends light back exactly where it came from.

      Observatories still fire lasers at it today. Thanks to these mirrors, we know the Moon is moving away from Earth by about 3.8 cm every year.
